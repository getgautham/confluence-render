# Wholesale outreach playbook

How we find stores that should stock us, reach them, and turn a reply into a first order.

## Who we sell to

| Store type | Why they buy | First order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Independent grocers | Local, premium, rotate shelves often | 2 to 3 cases |
| Health food stores | Customers ask for clean labels | 1 to 2 cases |
| Cafes with retail shelves | Impulse buys at the counter | 1 case |

## The weekly rhythm

1. **Monday:** pick 30 stores that look like our best stockists.
2. **Tuesday to Thursday:** send a short, personal first email to each.
3. **Friday:** follow up once with anyone who opened but didn't reply.

> Rule of thumb: one store, one sentence about *them*, one ask. Never a template blast.

## What a good first email looks like

```text
Hi Green Pantry team,

Saw you stock local granola next to the register. Ours is made in
Byron, 30 km from you. Could I drop off a sample box this week?

Andrew
```

## When they reply

- **"Send a price list"** → reply within the hour, attach the sheet, offer a sample.
- **"Not right now"** → thank them, set a reminder for 90 days.
- **"Yes"** → book the first order, then add them to the stockist map.

## Checklist before a store goes live

- [ ] Order confirmed in writing
- [ ] Delivery date set
- [ ] Shelf photo requested for the stockist map
